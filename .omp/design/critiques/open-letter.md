# An Open Letter to Open-Minded Progressives — critique (ArtDirector)

Spec: `books/open-letter/design/directions.md` + `art-direction-draft.md`
+ `chapters-draft.json` (all 14 chapters, 4 cohorts).
Baseline: capture #14 (ruled ledger + red glow, centered shell);
designer's own baseline notes (ch 9 flagship undistinguished) confirmed.

## Verdict: DIRECTION 1 ("BLACK-LETTER BROADSHEET") APPROVED, with binding conditions

The pick is right. Pamphlet wars are the book's native genre (Jacobites,
dissolution of the monasteries, "what is to be done"), the dissolving-
facade signature gives all four cohorts one idea with fourteen
variations, and the Direction 2/3 fold-ins (seal/salutation subsystem;
ch 10 ledger band) show good design economy. The Saul Bass "one idea per
title" discipline and the honest broadsheet-default self-critique show
the designer knows exactly which cliff they're walking. Proceed to
supervisor adoption + prototype; cohorts build only after prototype review.

## The three questions, answered

(a) **Blackletter legibility: approved at display scale with guards.**
UnifrakturCook 700 at ≥2rem for titles only is the right call — but:
titles must be sentence-cased book titles (never forced uppercase —
blackletter caps at 3rem are unreadable), line-height ≥1.06, max 22ch
with balanced wrap, and the fallback stack (Cloister Black → Old
English → serif) must be verified to still read offline. Prototype
review reads every one of the 14 titles at 390px: any title that scans
as texture rather than words gets EB Garamond 600 small-caps treatment
instead (keep this fallback designed, not improvised).
(b) **Distinct from Gentle Intro's arches: YES, by action.** Gentle
Intro's Cathedral is contemplative architecture (nested arches, candle);
Open Letter's facade is architecture MID-DISSOLUTION (shedding columns,
being uninstalled). Static Gothic vs Gothic coming apart — hold that
action line in every plate and the two Moldbug books rhyme without
blurring. The white cockade seal is Open Letter's alone.
(c) **Palette/motion: two flags.** Signal Red #D64533 at small sizes
must meter ≥4.5:1 wherever it touches text (the spec's own rule —
enforce it); Oxblood #7E2A22 large fields must not go muddy on Press
Black (prototype proves). Motion: the 1.1s plate settle + cockade stamp
are within the one-moment rule — but the stamp must not replay on every
chapter navigation (cover only, once per visit).

## Binding conditions

- **O1 — Ch 9 is the flagship or the book fails.** 520px facade mid-
   dissolution, oxblood-dominant, the most ambitious plate in the book.
   Prototype review opens ch 9 FIRST; if it doesn't dwarf its siblings
   in ambition, REVISE.
- **O2 — Plates fixed across themes (as spec'd).** Real jackets don't
   change with room lighting; verify the fixed plates stay elegant on
   Vellum (dark plates on paper + Newsprint-ink captions must not wash).
- **O3 — Salutation treatment is exact.** "Dear open-minded progressive,"
   in Garamond 500 + margin cockade, chs 4–14 ONLY where the text
   actually opens so (verify per chapter against JSON — never decorate
   a chapter that doesn't salute).
- **O4 — Redaction bars are content, not vibes.** One redaction bar per
   spread maximum; each must redact something (a name, a date, a number
   from the chapter's argument) — decorative black bars fail review.
- **O5 — Plex Mono registry note.** Open Letter's Plex is folio-data
   (numerals, counts, datelines) — small-scale, distinct from IJ's
   display-scale document voice and WtP's apparatus. Granted; no further
   Plex grants after this without exceptional cause.
- **O6 — Prototype scope.** Cover + chs 1/3/5/9/10/14 at three viewports
   before cohorts expand.
