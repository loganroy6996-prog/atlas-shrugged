## Binding conditions

- **Z1 — Gold-echo firewall (strictest in the library).** Solar gold
   #E8A93D on lapis is one step from the rejected "gold glow on dark."
   ALL gold must be FLAT engraved line-work (ray-fields, seals, rules
   via SVG paths) — zero radial/conic glow discs, zero `text-shadow`
   halos on Cinzel titles. If any opener reads luminous rather than
   incised, REVISE.
- **Z2 — Cinzel boundary with Twilight.** Both books now hold
   inscriptional caps. Separator: Zarathustra's Cinzel is LITURGICAL
   (balanced-wrap inscriptions, seal legends, pull-lines, song crowns —
   words performing ritual); Twilight's is MONUMENTAL (short verdicts,
   strike emblems, bronze tablets). If the prototypes read confusably
   close, Zarathustra leans into the seal/serpent/song apparatus —
   never into bronze-tablet minimalism, which is Twilight's.
- **Z3 — The Roundel must be magnificent.** The Sun-Serpent Roundel
   (≥40vmin, rayed disc + coiled serpent, engraved hand) is the single
   most important SVG in this book. It gets its own prototype-review
   verdict: crude serpent anatomy or clip-art rays fail the book.
- **Z4 — Pull-lines are verbatim + cited, every one.** Real prose with
   §/paragraph citation, chosen by cohort leads who READ the chapter.
   No invented epigraphs, no paraphrase passed off as quotation.
- **Z5 — p3c16's seven seal-rounds must read sevenfold.** The Ring of
   Recurrence refrain needs seven distinct round seals, not one seal
   repeated — validators count.
- **Z6 — Ember pivot at p4c10 is exact.** Part IV runs ember→gold with
   the pivot AT p4c10 (the golden ring vision). Chapters before p4c10
   ember-led, after gold-led; p4c10 itself the still pivot (quietest
   opener in the part — hold that against the temptation to gild it).
- **Z7 — Chapter-end slot.** Until ReaderLead rules on `#chapter-end`,
   the 24px mini-seal closes via the CSS `p:last-child::after` SVG-url
   override (authored art, acceptable) — never a font glyph.
