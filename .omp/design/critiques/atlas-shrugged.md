## Verdict: DIRECTION A ("THE SIGNAL") APPROVED, with binding conditions

The pick is right and the submission is excellent: the railroad-night
world is textually overwhelming (rails/track in 29/30 chapters by
census), the three signal aspects map the parts' dramatic logic at a
glance (clear/caution/stop = the Line's logic), the census-keyed plate
scenes prove 30× variation without ornament fatigue, and the p3c7
speech apparatus treats the 37,525-word address with gravity instead of
heroics. The sketches confirm it: A's night-signal plate + aspect bar +
three part plates read designed; B's specimen note is delicious but
frames rather than scenes; C's departure board is witty but symmetric-
facade-adjacent and comic at the climax. Both rejections are correctly
and non-portably argued (B starves the railroad sublime; C trivializes
the valley and the speech). Proceed to ART-DIRECTION.md + prototype
(cover + p1c8 + p2c7 + p3c7 + p3c10); cohorts build only after prototype
review.

  Clear Green #3FD08C / Order Amber #E8A33D / Stop Red #E0483E at
  ~3–4:1 on Nightballast: lamps, plates, rules, kickers ≥0.72rem
  tracked — never body or small text. Validators meter every opener.

  p1c10 STOP-on-green (Wyatt's Torch) + p3c1/p3c10 green-on-red

  wedges.** The anti-generic note already killed gradient glows — hold
  it in every one of the 30 plates. A single radial-glow lamp fails.
- **AT4 — Gold refusal is total.** No gold/brass/gilt anywhere (A2 by
  refusal). C's Brass frieze stays dead; B's dollar plates inside A
  use Intaglio Green line-work, never gilt.
- **AT5 — p3c7 speech apparatus, not hero.** Standard plate + Space
  Mono section numerals at speech beats + margin progress spine +
  relief spacing. Prototype MUST show a mid-address spread (not just
  the opener) proving rhythm and progress legibility across 37k words.
- **AT6 — Opener scale weights are authored per chapter.** Standard
  9rem / grand 12rem / monumental 13rem + inscription assigned by the
  manifest table (paras + words + content), never by threshold formula

  condensed grotesque caps; Atlas's Barlow must read TIMETABLE
