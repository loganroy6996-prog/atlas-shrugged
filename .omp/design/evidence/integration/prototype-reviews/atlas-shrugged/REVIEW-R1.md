## Position: Direction A (THE SIGNAL) is the right choice — endorse, with R2 conditions

- **A · THE SIGNAL (recommended):** "A novel in three signals" — green/amber/red aspect bar as the
  part system (I CLEAR / II CAUTION / III STOP), signal-gantry cover plate ("SIG 0-00 · CLEAR · THE
  LINE RUNS"), Barlow Condensed + Space Mono + Crimson Pro. Viewed: the strongest of the three by a
  distance. The signal grammar is native to the book's railroad sublime AND its moral logic (clear /
  contradictory / stopped world); the three-aspect bar gives 30 chapters a real system, not a tint
  cycle. Title-swap: pass — no other book on the shelf is a railroad novel with a three-part moral
  descent. The per-part plates (open track / signals-against-signals / stopped world with one green
  thread to the valley) show the system already thinking in parts, not pages.
- **B · THE CURRENCY (rejected finalist):** banknote/specimen grammar. Handsome (viewed), and the
  verdict note is honest — it starves Part I's railroad sublime and 30 lathe borders would fatigue.
  Correctly kept as the dollar-plate detail inside A (the right burial: money peaks in II× and III,
  it must not lead). Endorse rejection.
- **C · THE TERMINAL (rejected):** departure-board grammar. The verdict note is right again — the
  board trivializes the valley and the Galt address, and the symmetric facade is closest to baseline
  DNA. Endorse rejection.

## Conditions for R2 (binding — do not expand before proving these)

1. **A must survive the non-railroad chapters.** The signal grammar is perfect for the Line, the
   Taggart offices, and the decline arc — but Part III contains the valley (pastoral utopia) and
   "THIS IS JOHN GALT SPEAKING" (60-page radio address). R2 must show how A treats at least one
   valley chapter and the Galt address WITHOUT becoming a generic dark page with a green bar. If the
   answer is "one green thread — the valley" (as the part-III plate hints), prove it rendered.
2. **The aspect bar must not become a repeated ornament.** 30 chapters × the same 3-segment bar =
   the exact repeated-motif system the relaunch rejects. R2 must show the bar's per-chapter variation
   grammar (aspect per chapter? position? occlusion? mono SIG captions like "SIG 0-08 · THE LINE
   RUNS"?) with three neighboring chapters side by side proving they share grammar, never pixels.
3. **Wire it.** R2 is a wired prototype (real page + book.css + manifest + real chapters), not more
   sketches — same bar as every other book. Suggested R2 set: cover + p1c1 (Clear) + p2c10 (Caution
   peak) + p3c1 (valley) + p3c7 (Galt address) at all three widths, plus the TOC drawer showing the
   signal system across 30 rows.
