# Usage policy — active from the user's latest direction

The user said usage is too high and requested continued aggressive progress at a somewhat slower pace. This policy supersedes the original instructions to launch every book and every specialist simultaneously. Preserve the creative quality the user approved on the homepage and use Step 5 Preview Free at the highest effort offered by the active native client (OpenCode: high).

- Work on at most two books actively at a time, alongside necessary shared integration. Finish existing designs, implementation and review before starting another book wave.
- Project task concurrency is 6; the global background-job ceiling is 16. This is a ceiling, not a target. Keep at least four job slots available for required specialist work and integration. Waiting supervisors should park and free slots.
- Reuse existing agents, accepted art directions, research, manifests and assets. Do not recreate the hierarchy, repeat complete skill/brief reads on every repair, or launch fresh discovery for settled choices.
- Existing supervisors may perform bounded implementation work themselves. Spawn a specialist only for a concrete missing deliverable; independent visual review remains required. Batch review of related fixes.
- No mass broadcasts, progress probes, repeated roster censuses or cross-agent status chatter. Report when a tangible milestone lands or a real blocker changes. Coordinate through concise local artifacts when no response is needed.
- Use local scripts for deterministic file/manifest/asset checks. Reuse successful baseline evidence. Take new screenshots for implementation changes and review findings; avoid duplicate capture rounds of unchanged pages.
- Per-agent soft request budget is 120 (previously 400), with normal wrap-up behavior. Yield useful files and a focused handoff when approaching it; do not treat the budget as a spending target.
- Transient API errors get at most two automatic retries with a 5-second base backoff. No model/provider fallback or expansion to paid capacity. A hard usage-limit response must stop that wave and be reported with its reset time; do not restart every supervisor into repeated 429 errors.
- Continue the full thirteen-book scope in smaller waves. Do not weaken the artistic or functional acceptance criteria, or call a queued book completed.

Before launching a new wave, read the current project config and this policy. Make the remaining-work queue explicit, pick the next two books, and bring them through integration and review. Do not respawn all thirteen supervisors on recovery.
