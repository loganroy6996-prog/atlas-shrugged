# Art Direction — The Power Broker: Robert Moses and the Fall of New York

## Identity

Concrete slate, transit-map blue, and asphalt black. The cover’s city grid and road lines evoke infrastructure as a form of power.

## Cover and chapter plates

Use street grids, elevated roads, and compressed skyline silhouettes. The seven printed parts remain distinct in navigation; chapter art should feel infrastructural, not celebratory.

## Reader integration

- Keep the cover artwork in `art/cover.svg` and its `design/chapters.json` entry on the shared reader art slot.
- Chapter entries use the stable chapter IDs in `data/toc.json`; captions and motifs must stay attached to those IDs.
- Keep contrast, proportions, and chapter typography consistent with the existing `theme.css` and shared reader controls.
