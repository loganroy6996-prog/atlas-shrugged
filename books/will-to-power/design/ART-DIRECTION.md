# The Will to Power — Art Direction: "The Notebooks of the 1880s"

Book: *The Will to Power* (Friedrich Nietzsche, posthumous 1901; this edition:
"Selections from the Notebooks of the 1880s"). 52 chapters, 5 parts, ~183k words.
Companion contract: `book-identity.json` (tokens, fonts, per-part grammar,
chapter-opener composition contract — normative for cohort teams).

## 1. What this book actually is (grounding)

Sampled in full or in opening + middle passages: p1c1, p2c2, p2c5, p3c4, p3c10,
p4c5, p4c15, p5c3, p5c7. Structural census across all 52 chapter JSON files.

- **It is not a book; it is a quarry.** Posthumous selections from working
  notebooks: numbered fragments, `(a)/(b)` sub-notes, `NB.` margin-cries (46
  paragraphs carry them), aphorisms from one line to 500 words. Median paragraph
  length is 40–60 words; p5c3 holds 198 paragraphs, p3c10 holds 168. The design
  must honor the *fragment* as the unit of thought — long, calm reading broken
  by short, struck utterances.
- **Its arc is demolition then legislation.** Book I (European Nihilism):
  diagnosis — "there is no goal, no answer to the question: why?", the eternal
  recurrence as "the most terrible thought". Book II (Critique of the Highest
  Values): the hammer — religion, morality, philosophy each cross-examined
  (p3c4's Christianity polemic runs 159 paragraphs). Book III (New Determination
  of Values): the laboratory — knowledge, nature, life, society rebuilt from the
  will to power (p4c15, 137 paragraphs of biology-as-philosophy). Book IV
  (Discipline and Cultivation): the ascent — rank, the strong, the noble man,
  "the highest man as legislator of the future". The Preface promises "the
  history of the next two centuries" and "music of the future".
- **Its voice is violent and intimate at once.** "What has greatness must be
  spoken of with greatness, cynically and without shame, or not at all."
  Lightning metaphors, hammer metaphors, medical metaphors (sickness, décadence,
  quarantine), legal metaphors (legislators, values, rank). Recurring images:
  the ring/circle (recurrence), the broken tablet (values), the eye and the
  microscope (perspectivism), the ladder and the mountain (rank).

The rejected baseline ("forge press": crimson gradients, rail rules, numeral
badges, skew/opacity churn per chapter) fails because it is *industrial*, not
*editorial*: it treats Nietzsche like a metalworks and the fragment like a
railway timetable. The new identity treats him like what he is — a dying
philosopher writing numbered lightning-flashes in notebooks, posthumously bound.

## 2. References studied (with citations)

 studied via web, 2026-10-15; principles extracted, not pixels copied.

1. **Nietzsche Source DFGA facsimile edition** (nietzschesource.org/DFGA) — the
   actual 1880s notebooks: dense ink, marginal insertions, fragment numbers,
   ruled pages. Lesson: the *numbered fragment on a ruled field* is the book's
   native visual unit. Our fragment numerals and margin rules come from here.
2. **Walter Kaufmann / R. J. Hollingdale Vintage edition** (Random House, with
   "Facsimiles of the Original Manuscript" — archive.org copy inspected) —
   restraint: the fragments carry the weight; the apparatus stays quiet. Lesson:
   calm body, loud openers.
3. **Edvard Munch's Nietzsche portraits** (1905–1906, Munch Museum MM.M.00254 /
   MM.T.01657; WikiArt "Friedrich Nietzsche, 1906") — crayon/pen, melancholy
   blacks with sickly green-yellow grounds. Lesson: the palette's *sulphur and
   bone* register; darkness that is chemical, not neutral.
4. **German Expressionist woodcut** (MoMA technique essay; NGA teaching packet
   on Kirchner/Nolde; Emil Nolde, *Prophet*, 1912) — gouged black planes, white
   left as violent negative space, hand-cut irregularity. Lesson: our
   illustration medium — authored SVG plates that *gouge* (knockout masks, torn
   edges), never gradients-behind-text.
5. **Penguin Classics Nietzsche covers** (penguin.co.uk Nietzsche author page) —
   single strong image + severe typography. Lesson: one signature element per
   surface; TOC and cover each get one idea, not five.

Plus typographic memory: 19th-century German philosophical title pages (spaced
capitals, roman numerals as architecture) and broadsheet "tablets of the law"
composition for part openers.

## 3. Three directions considered

### Direction A — "The Black Notebook" (CHOSEN)
A posthumous archival object: ink-black paper, bone-white ink, one
sulphur-gold accent like a match struck in a dark room. Fragments carry big
hand-cut numerals; `NB.` cries get margin treatment; each part gets an authored
woodcut-gouge SVG plate (ring / hammer-tablet / eye / ladder-mountain) in a
per-part ink color. Display type is Fraunces in its blackest, tightest optical
size; body is Newsreader; apparatus is IBM Plex Mono.
Signature: *the fragment as exhibit* — the chapter numeral rendered as a large
gouged plate-mark, and per-part woodcut motifs.

### Direction B — "Lightning over Sils-Maria" (rejected)
A Romantic-alpine treatment: stormy mountain nocturne palette (deep blue-black,
electric violet, ice white), huge italic display, animated lightning chapter
dividers. Considered because the eternal recurrence and the "highest man"
passages invite the sublime. **Why not:** it turns a philosopher's working
notebook into a fantasy novel; the alpine sublime fits *Zarathustra* (mountains,
caves, sun) far better than this book of numbered fragments; lightning dividers
would repeat identically across 52 chapters and become the exact repeated-motif
system the relaunch rejects. It also fails the title-swap test: the same design
would suit any "dark epic" book unchanged.

### Direction C — "The Laboratory Ledger" (rejected)
A clinical-scientific treatment: cold slate grounds, tabular numerals,
specimen-plate diagrams, thin rules, "experiment log" chapter headers.
Considered because Book III is genuinely biological (protoplasm, nutritive
processes, "Biological Value of Knowledge"). **Why not:** it fits only one of
four books; Books I, II, and IV are prophetic, polemical, and legislative, not
clinical. A ledger aesthetic would also flatten the book's real emotional
extremes (terror, contempt, exaltation) into a single cool register, and its
thin-rule/tabular grammar is the closest of the three to the generic
"SaaS-card-kit / broadsheet" defaults the frontend-design skill warns against.

**Comparison summary:** A is *editorial and archival* (the book as found
object); B is *pictorial and sublime* (the book as landscape); C is *systemic
and clinical* (the book as dataset). Only A spans all five parts (preface-plate
→ gloom → fracture → lens → ascent), honors the fragment unit found in every
chapter, and passes the title-swap test below.

## 4. The chosen identity: "The Black Notebook"

### Title-swap test
Replace the title with *Beyond Good and Evil* or *The Gay Science* and this
design must feel wrong. It does: the gouged fragment-numerals, the `NB.`
margin-cries, the woodcut plates keyed to recurrence/tablet/eye/ladder, and the
"posthumous notebook, bound after death" apparatus belong to a *fragment
collection*, not to the polished aphorism-books. BGE wants epigrams and masks;
GS wants songs and science; WtP wants exhibits and evidence. The design says
"here lie the numbered remains" — swap the title and it lies.

### Type system (all verified loadable, 2026-10-15)
- **Display — Fraunces** (Google Fonts, variable `ital,opsz,wght`). Roles: cover
  title, chapter-opener titles, part numerals. Black weights (700–900),
  `opsz` 144 for display, tight tracking (−0.02em), line-height 0.95–1.0. Its
  wonky, hand-cut old-style axes feel engraved, not corporate.
- **Body — Newsreader** (Google Fonts, variable `ital,opsz,wght`). Roles: all
  reading prose, TOC chapter names. Light-to-regular (300–450), `opsz` ~18,
  line-height 1.65–1.75 for serif body, measure 34–38rem (~65–72 chars).
  Slightly inky, literary, calm at length.
- **Apparatus — IBM Plex Mono** (Google Fonts, 400/500). Roles: fragment
  numerals, `NB.` markers, part eyebrows, meta strings (word counts, "§"
  markers), buttons/labels. Never for prose. Letterspaced +0.08em at small
  sizes.
  `https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Fraunces:ital,opsz,wght@0,9..144,300..900;1,9..144,300..700&family=Newsreader:ital,opsz,wght@0,6..72,300..600;1,6..72,300..600&family=IBM+Plex+Mono:wght@400;500&display=swap`
  (Contract note: READER-CONTRACT §8 prefers book-relative `@font-face` in
  `design/fonts/` so the build ships fonts; the Google Fonts `<link>` above is
  the verified reference load and the acceptable remote fallback. Supervisor:
  vendor the woff2 files if offline parity is required.)
- Fallbacks: Display `Georgia, 'Times New Roman', serif`; body `Georgia,
  'Iowan Old Style', serif`; mono `ui-monospace, 'SF Mono', Menlo, monospace`.

### Scale
Cover title `clamp(3.5rem, 11vw, 7rem)`; chapter-opener title
`clamp(2.2rem, 5.5vw, 3.6rem)`; part numeral `clamp(4rem, 10vw, 7rem)` Fraunces
900; body 1.0625–1.125rem; fragment numeral 0.8rem mono (margin) with 2.6rem
opener plate-mark; eyebrows 0.72rem mono tracked.

### Layout & composition
- **Cover:** full-viewport archival plate. Left-aligned monumental Fraunces
  title ("THE / WILL TO / POWER"), a large gouged ring-and-hammer SVG plate
  (authored, knockout mask) occupying the right 40% on desktop / stacked above
  title on phone; mono eyebrow "FRIEDRICH NIETZSCHE · NOTEBOOKS OF THE 1880s";
  sulphur rule; quiet ghost buttons. No gradient washes; depth comes from the
  plate's gouge texture and one soft vignette.
- **Chapter opener (contract, see JSON):** (1) mono part eyebrow
  (`BOOK II — CRITIQUE OF THE HIGHEST VALUES HITHERTO`); (2) gouged part
  plate-mark (per-part motif, ~120px, left of or above title); (3) Fraunces
  chapter title; (4) thin sulphur rule with fragment-count inscription
  (`198 FRAGMENTS · 11,975 WORDS`); (5) drop cap in Fraunces 900 on first
  paragraph. Short chapters (p3c1, p4c1, p5c1 — single spaced-title paragraphs)
  render as *tablet openers*: title set huge, centered, no drop cap, plate-mark
  above.
- **Reading body:** calm single column, Newsreader, generous margins. Fragments
  separated by extra paragraph rhythm (no visible divider except a small
  sulphur `·` fleuron at authored boundaries — never modulo-generated: cohort
  JSON lists exact paragraph indices). `NB.` paragraphs get a sulphur left
  margin-bar + mono "NB." marker via the frozen shared hook
  `#chapter-text > p.reader-nb` (READER-CONTRACT §7; reader.js applies it when
  a paragraph starts with `NB.`/`NB `/`NB:`/`N.B.` — no book JS needed).
  Fragment-count inscriptions use `#chapter-text[data-paras]` +
  `reader:chapter.detail` (contract §2/§7), never hardcoded counts in CSS.
- **TOC:** part headers as "book plates" — roman numeral in Fraunces 900 at
  2rem + subtitle in mono, separated by real whitespace (no hairline-grid
  wallpaper); chapters listed with mono numerals in the part ink and Newsreader
  titles; the current chapter marked with a sulphur margin bar.
- **Navigation:** prev/next as quiet "← Previous fragment-book / Next →" with
  chapter titles; progress bar thin sulphur; header shows part + chapter in
  mono. Settings keep shared behavior; theme default dark "Inkblack".

### Illustration medium
Authored inline SVG "gouge plates": black-on-transparent shapes with
knockout-mask texture (irregular hand-cut edges, hatch gouges), tinted per part
(part ink at 85% + Sulphur accents). Plates load DECLARATIVELY through the
frozen art slots (READER-CONTRACT §3–§4): cover plate → `#cover-art` via
manifest `cover.src: "art/cover.svg"`; chapter plates → `#chapter-art` via
per-chapter `src` + `class` + `motif` (motif also sets `body[data-art]` for
motif-keyed CSS). Cohort teams declare entries in `design/<cohort>.json`;
supervisor merges sequentially into `design/chapters.json`. One master
vocabulary, five plates:
- Part 0 (Preface): **the sealed envelope / unbroken tablet** — Pencil + Bone.
- Part I: **the broken ring** (eternal recurrence cracking) — Gloom Violet.
- Part II: **the shattered tablet / hammer-fracture** — Bloodline.
- Part III: **the lens-eye / cell** (perspectivism, protoplasm) — Verdigris.
- Part IV: **the ladder / ascending steps to a star** — Heights Blue.
Cover plate: ring + hammer combined composition. Cohort teams author chapter
plate-marks *within* this grammar (rotate/crop/detail motifs per chapter
rationale in their cohort JSON); they MUST NOT invent new media (no gradients,
no emoji, no stock). Chapter plate placement follows the part-grammar slots in
`book-identity.json` (e.g. Book III right-float on wide screens) and MUST NOT
shift body-text rhythm when the slot is empty (`[hidden]` slots stay
`display:none` per contract §3).

### Per-part variation (the system, not a tint cycle)
| Part | Ink | Motif | Opener mood | Body distinction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 Preface | Faint Pencil | sealed tablet | hushed, centered, small plate | standard calm |
| I European Nihilism | Gloom Violet | broken ring | heavy, low, ring large behind eyebrow | fragment numerals violet |
| II Critique… | Bloodline | shattered tablet | aggressive, plate-mark overlaps title edge | NB. bars bloodline; blockquotes bloodline edge |
| III New Determination | Verdigris | lens-eye/cell | curious, plate-mark right-floated on wide screens | fragment numerals verdigris; `(a)/(b)` sub-notes indented with green tick |
| IV Discipline… | Heights Blue | ladder/star | airy, generous top space, ascending | opener rule becomes stepped (3 ascending steps) |

### Motion
One orchestrated moment: cover plate draws in (stroke/gouge reveal, ≤900ms,
`prefers-reduced-motion` disables). Chapter transitions: none beyond shared
fade. No scroll-jacking, no per-card reveals.

2. Author your cohort SVG plate(s) in `art/<cohort>.svg` following the

   `motif` fields per READER-CONTRACT §4 schema for merging into

4. Keep the reading body calm: Bone on Inkblack Newsreader, no art behind prose,
5. Prototype your cover-adjacent openers, screenshot at 1440×1000 / 820×1180 /
   390×844, and record evidence honestly.
6. Meet the §8 benchmark addendum on every surface you touch: each control,
   ending, and marginal detail must show Black-Notebook authorship, not the
   shared default with a palette swap.

## 8. Benchmark addendum — homepage-level authorship on every surface
User benchmark (`.omp/design/USER-FEEDBACK.md`): the new homepage's creativity
is the bar "Everywhere", expressed through EACH BOOK'S OWN identity — never by
copying the homepage's look. Main + ArtDirector will side-by-side prototype
review and REVISE weak generic shells.
The reading body stays calm; ambition shows in composition, marginalia, and
controls. Normative details for WtP:
- **Chapter endings:** every chapter closes with an authored end-mark in the
  part ink: Preface a small sealed diamond; Book I a broken-ring arc gap;
  Book II a double fracture slash; Book III a lens-dot (ring + center point);
  Book IV three ascending steps. Cohort JSON declares the mark per chapter
  (same mark across a part, scaled down for tablet chapters) — never a shared
  default ornament, never modulo-chosen.
- **Progress & resume:** thin Sulphur progress bar with a gouge-textured
  leading edge (2–3px torn mask, not a glow); cover "Continue" line set in
  Plex Mono with the saved chapter's part ink numeral + Sulphur arrow.
- **Prev/next:** quiet labeled cards: mono "PREVIOUS FRAGMENT-BOOK" /
  "NEXT FRAGMENT-BOOK" eyebrows in the ADJACENT chapter's part ink, chapter
  titles in Newsreader, Sulphur hairline on the side facing travel direction;
  Book IV's next-card carries the ladder tick.
- **Header:** part + chapter in Plex Mono; the part numeral takes the part
  ink; header rule is a 1px Faint-Pencil line with a 24px Sulphur segment at
  the reading position (mirrors progress without duplicating the bar).
- **Settings panel:** mono section labels, Sulphur selected states, part-ink
  neutral (settings are book-chrome, not chapter-content — no part ink inside
  the panel); font/size/leading rows keep shared behavior, Black-Notebook
  finish.
- **TOC drawer:** per §4 book plates + part-ink numerals; the `/` filter
  input gets a mono "SEARCH THE FRAGMENTS…" placeholder and Sulphur caret;
  per-book progress line in Sulphur; current chapter = Sulphur margin bar +
  part-ink numeral (both, deliberately redundant for scan + identity).
- **Translator numerals:** 201 paragraphs carry inline edition numerals
  ("themselves.1", "someone else.86") — provenance, not decoration. Set them
  as superscript Plex Mono Ash (0.72em, no Sulphur, no links — WtP has no
  notes apparatus: `notes_part` null, zero `note_refs`/`epigraph` keys in
  data). Never hide them, never enlarge them into marginalia.
- **NB.-heavy spread (W6 deliverable):** p3c4 carries 4 NB. paragraphs —
  the prototype spread stages one NB. cry with its margin-bar + marker at
  desktop measure so director review can judge the shout-vs-calm balance.
- **What stays out:** no endnote round-trips (no notes data), no generated
  epigraphs, no cover-art behind prose, no per-paragraph color rotation.
