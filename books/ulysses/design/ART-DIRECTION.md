# Art Direction — Ulysses

## Identity

Dublin blue, ink violet, and fog grey. The cover is a route through one city day, with street lines and ordinary objects carrying the Homeric scale.

## Cover and chapter plates

Preserve the three-part Telemachia, Odyssey, and Nostos grouping. Repeat the route-map frame across all eighteen episode plates and change the episode label. Keep the plate language consistent while the text itself shifts style from episode to episode.

## Reader integration

- Keep the cover artwork in `art/cover.svg` and its `design/chapters.json` entry on the shared reader art slot.
- Chapter entries use the stable chapter IDs in `data/toc.json`; captions and motifs must stay attached to those IDs.
- Keep contrast, proportions, and chapter typography consistent with the existing `theme.css` and shared reader controls.
