# Art Direction — Things Hidden Since the Foundation of the World

## Identity

Night violet, pale lavender, and chalk. Interlocking triangles express mimetic desire; a broken circle hints at the scapegoat mechanism and ritual order.

## Cover and chapter plates

Retain the three source books—Fundamental Anthropology, The Judaeo-Christian Scriptures, and Interdividual Psychology. Use abstract relational forms and avoid illustrating sacred figures.

## Reader integration

- Keep the cover artwork in `art/cover.svg` and its `design/chapters.json` entry on the shared reader art slot.
- Chapter entries use the stable chapter IDs in `data/toc.json`; captions and motifs must stay attached to those IDs.
- Keep contrast, proportions, and chapter typography consistent with the existing `theme.css` and shared reader controls.
