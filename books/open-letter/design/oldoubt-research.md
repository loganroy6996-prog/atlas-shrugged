## 6. Three visual directions (comparison)

**Direction A — "Contagion Atlas."** Epidemiological register: transmission-route maps (Fox-mosquito → X zombies; Times-milk → Y zombies), peloton drift charts, infection-tree diagrams. *Fits:* c1's virus theory and c4's peloton beautifully. *Fails:* c2's Naples/1914 set-pieces and c3's pendulum/clock have no disease content — forcing them into petri dishes would feel like exactly the pseudo-systemic decoration the contract bans. Also risks a cold, clinical read over 30k words.

**Direction B — "Empire Album."** Archival-travelogue register: sepia maps, harbor views (Naples), chancellery documents (Grey's memos), gold-reserve ledgers. *Fits:* c2 best of all three; strong material for the long quotations. *Fails:* collides head-on with sibling cohorts (RESTORE chose engraved plates; CATHEDRAL is working Piranesi/Gothic engraving) — three engraving systems in one book would read as one monotonous system, and DOUBT would lose its identity. Also weakest for c3's abstract theory and c4's taxonomy.

**Direction C — "Stitches in the Canvas" (CHOSEN).** Trompe-l'œil register: each chapter opens with a *painted* scene — a horizon, a harbor, a sky — rendered as visibly brushworked panels in SVG, each with its chapter-specific tear, stitch, or rip revealing the dark ground beneath. The cohort's own master image (canvas/curtain, §5.1) becomes its grammar: c1's yacht-rip, c2's slander-curtain, c3's clock-face crack, c4's single eye staring through the tear. Theory diagrams (pendulum, types 1/2/3, peloton) are drawn as *painted diagrams on the canvas* — chalk on scenery — so abstraction and scenery share one medium. *Why it wins:* (1) it belongs to this cohort alone — no sibling uses painted scenery, and no other catalog title has the canvas image; swapping in another book would feel wrong; (2) it scales across all four registers (stand-up, travelogue, theory, taxonomy) because every chapter gets both a scene and a rip; (3) the rip/tear gives the engineer a genuine SVG-craft brief (torn edges, stitch marks, canvas weave, revealed dark) that cannot collapse into gradients or pseudo-elements; (4) reading body stays calm — all drama lives in the opener plate + colophon, exactly as the contract wants.
