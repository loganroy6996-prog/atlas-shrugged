## 6. Three visual directions (comparison)

**Direction A — "Pulp Space Opera."** Fiction-magazine register: masthead openers, ray-gun ornament, starfields, duotone alien-planet vignettes (Urplat, Vega). *Fits:* c11's frame, c14's send-off. *Fails:* c12's historiography and c13's constitutional mechanics would read as costume — the philosophy would feel unserious. Pulp wants motion and shock; the cohort's core pleasures are diagrams and verdicts.

**Direction B — "Constructivist Program."** Political-poster register: diagonal axes, stencil numerals, M–Q dividers, laser-beam geometry, stamp/seal devices (Trust seal, 3VC mark). *Fits:* c13 best of all three; strong poster energy for "laser-like program." *Fails:* risks reading as the revolutionary propaganda the text opposes (irony too inside to land visually); poster systems skew abstract/cold over 400+ paragraphs of reading; closest to the rejected generic-badge/rail family if discipline slips.

**Direction C — "Night Plates: engraved restoration frontispieces" (CHOSEN).** White-line engraving (scraperboard) on dark grounds: each chapter opens with an authored etched plate — burin hatching, cross-cut shading, cartouches, seals — in the tradition of 18th/19th-century political-tract frontispieces (the cohort's own cited medium: Webster 1794, Pobedonostsev 1869, the 1956 Times, the pre-1922 corpus). Corrosion-vs-geometry becomes etched decay vs. ruled order. Pulp and poster motifs are *absorbed as engraved subjects* (an engraved Slime Beast; an engraved laser diagram; engraved pilot wings) rather than competing systems. *Why it wins:* (1) it belongs to this book — no other catalog title cites print artifacts so densely; swapping in another title would feel wrong; (2) white-on-dark line engraving is visibly ambitious at opener scale yet keeps body text calm; (3) it scales across the cohort's tonal range (axiom → diagnosis → program → send-off) via plate tone; (4) it is natively original-SVG work (paths/hatching), no gradients-as-art, no badges/rails. *Risk:* literal Piranesi density is infeasible in hand SVG — mitigate with stylized geometric plates (ruled lines, stipple fields, bold silhouettes) at controlled scales.
