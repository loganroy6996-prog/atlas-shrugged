## p1c8 — "A Reset Is Not a Revolution" (Chapter 8)

**Argument in brief.** Defines the Cathedral ("The Cathedral has two parts:
the accredited universities and the established press. The universities
formulate public policy. The press guides public opinion… It's as simple as
a punch in the mouth"), argues the Inner/Outer Party structure makes
progressive victory invisible to progressives themselves, rejects three
antiprogressive strategies (violence, Gramscian entryism, Fabian
democratism), and proposes the **reset**: lustrate the old regime, install
the new one in a single step, reward nobody who brought it about.

**Concrete images / passages to design from (all verbatim or close paraphrase):**

1. The Cathedral as "a true peer-to-peer network… no Leader, no Central
   Committee, no nothing" — resilient because decentralized. Network
   imagery, but 2008-era: the metaphor is organizational, not digital.
2. The Footlight Parade (1933) NIRA-eagle personality cult — "eagles shooting
   lightning bolts", group calisthenics displaying the Leader's face.
3. "speaking (quite literally) ex cathedra" — bylines as vestments; the NYT
   blackletter masthead as a logo of infallibility; "cardinals of the One,
   Holy, Catholic and Apostolic Church" vs "professors at Stanford".
4. "like trying to watch a circus through the camera on a cell phone
   duct-taped to the elephant's trunk" — the book's own media-ecology image.
5. The fascist gate to power: "closed, locked, welded shut, filled with a
   thousand tons of concrete, and surrounded by starving cave bears."
6. The Outer Party holding Queen-of-England power; Bush 2000–06 differing
   from a Democratic regime in exactly one policy (Iraq).
7. "Don't make the mistake of trying to sweep out the Augean stables. Just
   apply the river." — the lustration image.
8. The reset triad of computing metaphors: "just like rebooting your
   computer", "a little more like reinstalling Windows", "replace Windows
   with Linux" (the Stuart restoration).
9. "You can restore the Stuarts or not restore the Stuarts, but you can't
   restore 36% of the Stuarts." — the single-step principle as an
   anti-fraction device.
10. The Stuart restoration cast: Hans-Adam II / Prince Alois of Liechtenstein,
    13-year-old Prince Joseph Wenzel, the warming-pan legend, Lee Kuan Yew
    as consultant, Singapore as the worked example.

**Motif proposal — "The Restoration Warrant".** An engraved royal warrant /
proclamation plate: three numbered articles (lustration; no rewards to the
resetters; a single step), a hanging wax seal, and the struck-through "36%"
fraction device. The warrant is the chapter's thesis made physical — a reset
is one signed instrument, not a movement.

**Palette role.** Iron-gall ink + sealing-wax red lead; candle-gold only in

## p1c9 — "How to Uninstall a Cathedral" (Chapter 9) — FLAGSHIP

**Argument in brief.** The book's central metaphor fully unpacked:
progressivism as the living mainstream of Protestant Christianity (via
Hayes's 1916/1924 digest); the First-Amendment case that the Cathedral is an
established areligion; the Watson case as the doctrine of HNU enforced by
blasphemy law; the soft reset (separation of education and state) worked
through and rejected; the hard reset (nationalize, retire, liquidate, sink
the trademarks) with Tudor precedents. Closes: "And how could this be
accomplished? Obviously, it can't be."

**Concrete images / passages (verbatim or close paraphrase):**

1. Hayes digest: YMCA, Salvation Army, federations of churches, Canada's
   united churches; "Fundamentalists" vs "Progressive or Modernist" brethren
   contesting the Presbyterian churches. "Dogs and cats, living together!"
2. The dead-or-alive test: what is alive but unidentified "has become so
   pervasive that you do not distinguish between it and reality itself."
3. The areligion taxonomy: "a no-god tradition an areligion… unireligion…
   direligion… trireligion… polyreligion" — "someone has been tampering with
   the tools you use to think."
4. "until that religion manages to sneak God under the carpet, at which
   point go ahead, dudes" — the establishment-clause evasion.
5. **The Fundamentalens**: "a cute optical accessory that transforms all
   things Sunni into things Shia, and vice versa." Through it, "Harvard and
   Stanford and Yale are fundamentalist seminaries… emit Jesus-freak
   codewords… the way a baby emits fermented milk", while Bob Jones / Oral
   Roberts / Patrick Henry sing "Imagine" every morning.
6. "a progressive atheocracy — that is, a system of government based on an
   official areligion, progressivism."
7. "The Cathedral is the apotheosis of chutzpah. It is always poisoning its
   parents, then pleading for clemency as an orphan."
8. The Watson case: the "struggle session", Gene Expression firebrands, the
   Sakharov comparison, the red pill "We don't know", Gates's swallowed pill
   ("that awful wiry feeling in the glial cells").
9. "exercising their papal infallibility through the ordinary magisterium of
   Times Square"; the Torres Vedras fallback; the Cathedral's "central keep"
   fortified on not-A and not-B; evidence "not much stronger than the
   evidence for the Holy Trinity."
10. HNU as "a mutated and metastasized version of the Quaker doctrine of the

    botmasters"; "priests, preachers, professors, bishops, teachers,
    commissars or journalists — the botmasters will rule."
12. Soft-reset machinery: "Call it 99.9%" true pravda; Aufklärung; liquidate
    the schools ("XBoxes and PCP"); "applied work" ("The involvement of

    need to kill him" — dissolve, don't scratch.

    NGOs; campuses "attractive to developers"; sink the trademarks ("the
    former can't call it the New York Times nor the latter Harvard");
    "liquidate the reputation capital."
15. Precedents: "Henry VIII's dissolution of the monasteries… the
    suppression of the Jesuits… denazification."

**Motif proposal — "The Westwork in Dissolution, seen through the
Fundamentalens".** A full-width engraved Gothic westwork (twin towers, rose

## Three directions considered

**Direction 1 — "Dissolution Inventory" (CHOSEN).** Tudor-dissolution
engraving grammar: copperplate/woodcut linework, monastic inventory ledgers,
wax seals, struck warrants, guilloche certificates. Native to all three
chapters: c9 names the dissolution of the monasteries as its own precedent;
c8's reset is a lustration inventory; c10's receivership is paperwork
(ledger, shares, stubs). Escapes every generic-default cluster — not
broadsheet columns (the rejected baseline), not SaaS cards, not acid-on-black,
not cream-and-terracotta, not template chrome. Gives the flagship the largest
possible authored-SVG subject: a cathedral in stone.

**Direction 2 — "Sysadmin Gothic" (REJECTED).** Literalize the computing
register: terminal uninstall logs, boot sequences, package-dependency graphs
("Removing cathedral-common…"), kernel consoles. Content-grounded — the
chapters say "rebooting your computer", "thread table", "replace Windows
with Linux" — but terminal aesthetics are a documented generic tell
(monospace-as-decoration), and the failure mode is hacker-chic cliché that
flattens the book's 18th-century prose register into a joke. Kept only as a
trace: the power-cycle roundel in c8's warrant and the inverted tracery
inside c9's lens.

**Direction 3 — "Cathedral Life" (REJECTED).** Mid-century propaganda-magazine
pastiche from c10's Soviet-Life conceit and c8's NIRA-eagle footage: duotone
covers, constructivist layouts, masthead plates. Rejected on two counts:
loving pastiche of the propaganda magazine misreads a book written against
it, and photo-illustration grammar cannot be honestly authored in SVG —
duotone vector "photos" would look cheap next to real engraving. The magazine
trace survives only as c10's certificate typography (masthead-like ELBO
imprint).

**Why the choice belongs to THESE chapters.** The identity is drawn from the
cohort's own nouns: cathedrals, dissolutions, inventories, warrants, seals,
ledgers, share certificates, guilloche. Swap in any other book's chapters and
the system breaks — a warrant plate over love poetry would be absurd. That is
the test from the design skill, and this grammar passes it.

**Palette (dark; cohort layer over the book's oxblood tokens):**

| Name              | Hex       | Role                                              |
|-------------------|-----------|---------------------------------------------------|
| Dissolution Black | `#0e0b09` | cohort plate ground (deeper than book `--bg`)     |
| Vellum Ash        | `#211a15` | plate inner fields, ledger paper-dark             |
| Sealing Wax       | `#b23a2a` | seals, tags, struck text, inessential column      |
| Candle Gold       | `#d9a05a` | lens ring, seals' edge, essential column, ELBO    |
| Quire Parchment   | `#d8c092` | engraving linework at layered opacities           |
| Clerestory Blue   | `#4a6fa5` | stained glass ONLY (intact half, c9 plate)        |

**Type (proposal; engineer to verify availability + computed styles):**

- Display numerals / drop initials / warrant headings: **Pirata One**
  (Google Fonts, 400) — blackletter that nods to the NYT masthead discussed
  in c8 without imitating it; fallback `UnifrakturMaguntia, serif`.
- Titles / UI serif: **Cormorant Garamond** (already loaded by the book).
- Body: the shared quiet reader serif, unchanged — calm and accessible.
- Ledger figures (c10): tabular numerals (`font-variant-numeric:
  tabular-nums`), letterspaced small caps for column heads; no new mono face
  (avoids the monospace-label tell).

**Linework vocabulary (all original authored SVG + CSS):**

- Engraving fields built from `<pattern>` hatch sets (45° / 135° / stipple
  dots) in Quire Parchment at 8–28% opacity over Dissolution Black —
  depth without gradients.
- Contour strokes: 1.5–2.5px parchment strokes with round caps for
  architecture; 1px for ledger rules and guilloche (guilloche via
  overlapping `<path>` hypotrochoid-ish loops, hand-tuned, not generated
  noise).
- The Fundamentalens: double brass ring, inner glass disc with inverted
  tracery motif, Sunni/Shia glyph pair (☉ / ☽) at cardinal points —
  recurring marginal mark for c9 only.
- Wax seals: layered circles with irregular edge path + debossed letter
  (R for reset/receiver), sealing-wax fill with darker rim.
- No card grids, no gradient washes as decoration, no pseudo-element
  repeating rails, no numeral badges — the rejected baseline vocabulary
  stays out.

**Reading-body treatment.** Shared quiet system respected: justified serif
at the book's measure, drop initial in Pirata One for cohort openers only,
ledger/blockquote passages in c10 set with tabular figures. Art lives in the
opener plates and one marginal mark per chapter (warrant seal → lens →
Fnargl sceptre); it never intrudes into the text column.

**Responsive notes.** Plates are inline SVG with `viewBox` scaling: full
composition ≥900px; below that the ledger strips stack under the plate and
the lens device carries the c9 opener alone (title band stays legible at
390px). Reduced motion: plates are static engraving — no entrance animation
required beyond the book's existing settle.
