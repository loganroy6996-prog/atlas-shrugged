# Art Direction — A Portrait of the Artist as a Young Man

## Identity

Jesuit ivory, soot, and muted amber. The cover arch and rising flame frame Stephen’s gradual escape from family, church, and nation.

## Cover and chapter plates

Use the five chapter plates as a progression from enclosed childhood to an opened horizon. Keep the image language spare: thresholds, windows, and a single flame; avoid literal portraits.

## Reader integration

- Keep the cover artwork in `art/cover.svg` and its `design/chapters.json` entry on the shared reader art slot.
- Chapter entries use the stable chapter IDs in `data/toc.json`; captions and motifs must stay attached to those IDs.
- Keep contrast, proportions, and chapter typography consistent with the existing `theme.css` and shared reader controls.
