# The Sovereign Individual — Three Art Directions (comparison)

Book: `sovereign-individual` · 11 chapters (p1c1–p1c11), single Part I.
Baseline to surpass: centered gold-on-charcoal cover, Cormorant title, thin rule + diamond,
justified body, `○`-glyph numeral badges, orbital-ring gradients. Each direction below differs
in **composition, type system, and art medium** — not tokens.

References studied (all via web search this session):
- R1. Bank of Canada Museum, "The art of guilloche" (2020) —
  https://www.bankofcanadamuseum.ca/2020/07/the-art-of-guilloche —
  geometric-lathe rosettes, anti-counterfeit line work.
- R2. Metropolitan Museum of Art, coll. search 393961, Cyrus Durand banknote lathe-work
  ornament panel — https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/393961 —
  engraved border/cartouche vocabulary.
- R3. Beinecke Library, Portolan Charts highlight —

  https://www.cia.gov/legacy/headquarters/kryptos-sculpture/ —

## Direction 1 — SPECIE: The Private Banknote ✔ CHOSEN

**Thesis.** The book argues money will escape the state (cybermoney, denationalized
currency, Hayek). Banknote engraving is literally the art of *trust in money* — here
reissued by no state at all. Every chapter is a **denomination (I–XI) in a private
currency**; the cover is the specimen note off which all plates are struck.

- Palette (dark-first, flat inks, no gradient decoration):
  `Vault` #0B100E (ground) · `Bone` #ECE3CC (text) · `Specie` #D2A72C (gold) ·
  `Copper` #B26A3B (plate lines) · `Verdigris` #5DA88A (patina green) ·
  `Seal` #9E3B2E (oxblood seal red).
- Type: **Bodoni Moda** (Google Fonts, display Didone — engraved-modern voice) for
  titles/denominations · **Source Serif 4** (Google Fonts) calm reading body ·
  **IBM Plex Mono** (Google Fonts) microtext, denominations, data. Scale: display
  clamp(3–5.5rem); chapter title 2.4rem; body 1.125rem/1.75, ≤68ch.
- Layout: **asymmetric plate**. Opener = left engraved plate (rosette art, ~38%) +
  right title block; cover = full specimen note inside lathe frame; body a quiet
  single column; TOC a ledger with engraved rules.
- Medium: authored SVG intaglio — hypotrochoid/petal guilloche rosettes, lathe-work
  borders, cross-hatch shading, circular microtext. Flat strokes only.
- Signature: denomination system — numeral cartouches, "SPECIMEN / PRIVATE ISSUE /
  SERIES 1997 / CYBERSPACE MINT" lockups, assay-bar progress.
- Nav: ledger TOC (denomination numerals I–XI, engraved rules); prev/next as
  "plate" turners; progress = assay bar filling toward fine gold.
- Chapter variation: 11 distinct rosette geometries (petal count, layers, dash
  rhythm, ink) + one content motif each (millennium asymptote, strata, furrow,
  fallen capital, wall rubble, severed belt, knot-mesh, Pareto spire, broken
  shuttle, sundial, double seal).
- Why THIS book: no other book in the library is *about money itself*. Replacing
  title/color would leave a banknote system that fits nothing else. (Risk review:
  gold-on-dark echoes the rejected baseline's tokens, so the design spends its
  difference on **medium** — dense authored line-work vs. gradient orbs — and
  **composition** — asymmetric plates vs. centered stack. Four inks, not one.)

## Direction 2 — RHUMB: The Portolan Atlas (rejected)

**Thesis.** The book narrates a crossing between ages (San Giovanni 1495 → Berlin
1989 → the year 2000). Portolan charts were the working tools of an era when
sovereignty moved by sea and map. Each chapter is a **charted leg of a voyage out
of the nation-state**; the cover a *Mappa Mundi 1997–?* routing Westphalia → Cyberspace.

- Palette: `Abyss` #0A0F1E · `Vellum` #E8DFC4 · `Route` #C8452C (vermilion) ·
  `Brass` #C9A24B · `Kelp` #4E6E5D · `Fog` #8A93A6.
- Type: **IM Fell English + IM Fell English SC** (Google Fonts, 17th-c. Fell types)
  display/labels · **Spectral** (Google Fonts) reading body. Antique cartographic voice.
- Layout: full-bleed chart openers, rhumb fans radiating from the chapter windrose
  behind an offset title; 8% marginalia rail for motif captions; body on a dark
  vellum panel.
- Medium: authored SVG cartography — rhumb fans, windroses, hachured coastlines,
  dotted voyage tracks, red initial capitals.
- Signature: the voyage — per-chapter route segments that join into one track across
  the book; cover shows the whole passage.
- Nav: TOC as portolan itinerary table with "leagues" (word counts); progress as
  a ship's day-run along the route.
- Chapter variation: 11 windroses + one coastal vignette each.
- Why this book: megapolitics as navigation; "transcending locality" (ch. 7) as
  open sea. Rejected: gorgeous, but voyage metaphors fit travel and exile books
  better; the money thesis (the book's load-bearing object) goes unexpressed, and
  Fell-to-screen readability at small sizes is weaker than Bodoni/Source Serif.

## Direction 3 — CIPHER: The Basalt Dossier (rejected)

**Thesis.** The book runs on secrecy: crypto-anarchy, encrypted money, covert
agencies, ch. 11's "latter-day barbarians behind the scenes." Treat the text as a
**declassified file** — EXHIBIT I–XI stamped on basalt. Nods to Kryptos (R4) at CIA
Langley and the cypherpunk sampler (R5's mono-data vernacular).

- Palette: `Basalt` #0F0F12 · `Chalk` #E9E6DC · `Signal` #D24335 · `Brass` #B98A2F ·
  `Slate` #6E7681 · `Oxide` #7A2A22.
- Type: **Archivo Expanded** (Google Fonts, grotesk, exhibit headers) ·
  **Newsreader** (Google Fonts, text serif) body · **IBM Plex Mono** cipher bands/data.
- Layout: severe dossier — sticky left evidence rail (vertical index + seal state);
  openers as stamped file headers with redaction bars and over-struck numerals;
  hard rules, disciplined whitespace.
- Medium: mono ciphertext bands, redaction bars, rubber-stamp SVG seals, strike
  registration marks. Flat.
- Signature: declassification arc — seals progress intact → cracked → shattered
  across the eleven exhibits; cover = evidence slab with a wrapping cipher band.
- Nav: TOC as exhibit list with FILED/OPEN stamps; progress as declassification %.
- Chapter variation: per-chapter cipher-band pattern + seal state + one redaction
  motif tied to that chapter's covert actor.
- Why this book: the paranoia is real to the text (ch. 11 especially). Rejected:
  the black/red dossier look is the single most common "dark redesign" default and
  risks reading as generic thriller chrome; it also undersells the book's
  constructive half (ch. 7–8 on cybereconomy and earnings).

## Decision

**SPECIE wins**: strongest claim on the book's actual subject (money × sovereignty),
most original medium in this library (engraved line-work, not glow/gradients), and a
chapter system (denominations + rosettes) that scales honestly to all eleven chapters.
