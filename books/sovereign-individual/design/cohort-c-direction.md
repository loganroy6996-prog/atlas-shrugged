## 1. Three-direction comparison (Phase 1, pre-conform — recorded honestly)

Explored against the cohort brief (luddite sabotage vs networks / democracy's
dusk / cryptography + natural-economy predation) BEFORE the SPECIE conform notice.
Superseded — kept as the required record of comparison.

- **D1 "Dossier" (rejected).** Basalt-black file aesthetic: redaction bars,
  rubber-stamp seals, mono cipher bands; p1c9 = smashed terminal still-life, p1c10
  = ballot-box dusk gradient, p1c11 = key + ledger. Verdict: the single most common
  "dark redesign" default; undersells the book's constructive half; the identity
  designer independently rejected the same idea (CIPHER) for the same reason.
- **D2 "Relic" (rejected).** Museum-vitrine antiquarianism: Fell types, vellum
  panels, period artifacts (cropping shears, ballot urn, wax seal) as literal
  illustrations. Verdict: voyage/relic metaphors fit history books; the money
  thesis — this book's load-bearing object — goes unexpressed; literal artifact
  illustration is weaker than a system at chapter scale.
- **D3 "Torn flag → network" (prototyped, then WITHDRAWN as off-identity).**
  A flag dissolving into network threads (p1c9 drafted as `art/ch-p1c9.svg` and
  viewed live). Verdict: supervisor ruled it generic dossier art, not SPECIE
  intaglio; the file was deleted and does not ship. The withdrawal confirmed the
  choice: only the money thesis is ownable.

**Choice: conform to SPECIE.** Only this book is *about money itself* — its
denationalization, encryption, escape from the state's mint. Engraved money-art
with the state removed is its thesis drawn; a portolan or dossier system could
dress many books, a private banknote cannot. Cohort C = C-reckoning: "reaction,
twilight, and the criminal interregnum: what fills the vacuum," ink arc
copper/seal → copper/oxide → seal-red (manifest-draft.json §cohorts).

## 2. Chosen system (SPECIE grammar, cohort application)

- **Palette (flat inks, dark-first):** Vault #0B100E ground · Ledger #121915
  panels · Bone #ECE3CC text (13.9:1) · Muted #A89C7C (7.4:1) · Faint #6E654E
  microtext · Specie #D2A72C gold (display/denominations/assay, 7.1:1) · Copper
  #B26A3B plate lines (graphics/large only, 4.0:1) · Seal #9E3B2E stamps, p1c11
  lead (graphics only, 3.4:1). No gradients as decoration (S2 firewall); no
  Verdigris in this cohort (leads ch. 3 & 7 only, S3).
- **Type roles:** Bodoni Moda = display only (titles, denomination numerals IX /
  X / XI, dial numerals, motto italic); Source Serif 4 = ALL reading body incl.
  footnote tail; IBM Plex Mono = plate lockups, microtext rings, assay lines,
  ledger figures, datelines. At 390px bump Bodoni weight, never size (S6).
- **Opener composition (plate-left, per draft §3):** left rail (~38%, min 300px)
  holds the `#chapter-art` engraved plate SVG (class `plate`, inlined by
  reader.js from `design/chapters.json` src); right holds part line, Bodoni
  title, epigraph in copper rule frame, then body. Below 900px the plate becomes
  a full-width banner (max-height 320px) above the title; plates are NEVER hidden
  on phones (S1). Body: quiet single column on Vault, ≤68ch; drop cap (Bodoni,
  Specie) first paragraph only; section heads small-caps + short copper rule.
- **Rosette discipline (S4):** three genuinely distinct geometries — p1c9
  rectilinear 9-fold warp/weft minis (WARP/WEFT pair); p1c10 circular 10-fold
  halo guilloche band; p1c11 twin stacked seals (fine 11-lobe hypotrochoid vs.
  11-spike epitrochoid + dashed satellite). Petal counts 9/10/11 = IX/X/XI.
- **Microtext (S5):** every ring/legend is real, true text (series lockups,
  chapter-grounded phrases, real epigraph attributions) set in Plex Mono at
  ≥9.5px in plate units; nothing is glyph salad or lorem.
## 3. Per-chapter plate specs (as built — all three viewed rendered in Chromium)

Shared plate furniture (760×1080 viewBox, portrait): Vault ground; Specie outer

### Plate IX — The broken shuttle (`art/plate-p1c9.svg`, 97KB)

Content anchor: the Luddite rising 1811–12, paras ~398–407 (West Yorkshire
croppers, General Ludd's "Army of Redressers," the oath to "put down all
Machinery hurtful to Commonality"). Sub-caption: "WEST YORKSHIRE · 1811–12 ·
NO. IX".
- Loom: copper beams (x144–616) + side posts + Specie rosette studs; 18 warp
  threads; warps #2/5/7/11/13/16 severed — copper stubs ending in seal dots,
  loose ends curling seal-red; 9 woven weft rows (alternating over-under offsets,
  Specie) below the fell; jagged seal tear (2.0) across the fell line.
- Shuttle: enlarged boat-shuttle (translate 388,330, rotate 7°), copper outline
  over 0.72-opaque Vault fill, bobbin window + seal-red pirn with end ticks;
  jagged snap + 3 splinters + micro-chips in seal; lower fragment drifting
  (rotate 24°); copper motion ticks behind. Reads snapped-at-the-fell at a glance.
- S4 minis: WARP/WEFT rectilinear 9-fold rosettes (52×34, dual-phase copper)
  flank the IX cartouche (copper lead, seal inner rule).
- Assay: "ASSAYED 9 OF 11 · TWENTY-NINE THOUSAND WORDS". Footers: "THE BROKEN
  SHUTTLE · GENERAL LUDD · ARMY OF REDRESSERS" / IBM trade-card line
  ""SOLUTIONS FOR A SMALL PLANET" · IBM · TRADE CARD" (para 4 — the small-planet
  provocation the Luddites answer). Rationale for the engineer: reactionaries
  smash the machinery of the information age — staged literally, in engraving.

### Plate X — Sundial at dusk (`art/plate-p1c10.svg`, 88KB)

Content anchor: Quigley weapons-conditions (paras 2–6: cheap arms, amateur arms,
infantry) + Toffler "political expression of mass production" (para 22) + the
title's twilight. Sub-caption: "HOROLOGIUM · MASS DEMOCRACY · NO. X".
- Dial: copper face r165 + hairline; 10 hour lines (XII..IIII, Bodoni 14, bone)
  fanning from the foot with correct horizontal-dial spread; gold cross-hatched
  gnomon blade on the noon line with apex dot + APEX tag.
- Dusk shadow: seal-red hatched band from the apex escaping PAST the dial rim
  into the halo (democracy outliving its conditions, spilling past the instrument
  that measured it) — the plate's one dramatic gesture; slender, clipped by
  nothing, labelled "TWILIGHT · THE LONG SHADOW".
- S4 halo: 10-fold hypotrochoid guilloche TRIPLE weave (3 phases/amplitudes)
  clipped to the r174–236 band + outer copper ring — a full spirograph ring, not
  scallops. Motto in Bodoni italic under the dial (Toffler, attributed "AFTER
  TOFFLER · QUOTED IN THE CHAPTER" — true: para 22 quotes it).
- Cartouche X (copper lead). Assay: "ASSAYED 10 OF 11 · THREE CONDITIONS OF
  DEMOCRACY". Footers: "THE SUNDIAL AT DUSK · FRATERNAL TWIN OF COMMUNISM" /
  "CHEAP ARMS · AMATEUR ARMS · INFANTRY · AFTER QUIGLEY" (true: paras 3–5).

### Plate XI — The counterfeit seal (`art/plate-p1c11.svg`, 124KB, PROTOTYPE standard)

Content anchor: covert criminal capture of the state — "barbarians in disguise,"
  Body Snatchers Pod People (paras 3–4), Tanzi's "privatization of the state"
  (para 0), trust + cryptographic keys (para 228). Seal-red leads — the only
  chapter where red leads (S3 graphics-only respected: red never sets text).
- Two STACKED strikes (vertical: official y300, counterfeit y610, r128 each —
  separation, not overlap): 1ST/OFFICIAL in gold (fine 11-lobe hypotrochoid +
  ghost phase + copper inner weave; whole cipher-key centre; microtext ring
  "MINTED BY NO STATE · REDEEMABLE IN TRUST · HONOUR AMONG ASSOCIATES"); 2ND/
  COUNTERFEIT in seal-red (11-SPIKE epitrochoid + dashed hypotrochoid satellite —
  genuinely different geometry, S4; broken-key centre with dashed shank;
  microtext "LATTER-DAY BARBARIANS · IN DISGUISE · PRIVATIZATION OF THE STATE").
- S7 intentionality: shift-bridge between strikes ("2ND STRIKE · OFF REGISTER ·
  +0/+300") with strike arrows + 1ST/2ND labels; colophon finish — seal rule +
  "COLOPHON · THE COUNTERFEIT SEAL · END OF THE ELEVEN". Reads as climax, not
  printing error. Assay: "ASSAYED 11 OF 11 · FINE". Cartouche XI in seal red.

## 4. Reading treatments (for the engineer)

- **p1c9 sub-section transitions (29,489 words):** cycle three engraved ornaments
  across the 18 major ALL-CAPS heads in reading order (indices in
  cohort-c.json#heading_index_map): (a) shuttle-mark (snapped-shuttle glyph),
  (b) fell-rule (short seal tear-tick rule), (c) warp/weft mini-rosette
  (alternating WARP/WEFT plate geometry). No two adjacent transitions repeat. 37
  minor Title Case heads: quiet small-caps (real font-variant-caps, 0.8125rem,
  0.18em) + short copper rule only. 37 wrap fragments MUST NOT be head-styled
  (explicit not-heads list in the manifest). Selectors: unlayered
  `body[data-chapter="p1c9"] #chapter-text > p:nth-of-type(N)` — unlayered book
  CSS always wins over `@layer shared` (contract §8).
- **p1c10:** Quigley conditions (paras 3–5) as engraved assay table; major heads
  take marginal hour-tick ornaments; end-mark = single gnomon tick.
- **p1c11 appendix + footnote tail:** appendix paras 249–288 as numbered strategy
  ledger (mono clause numerals); tail from para 289 ("Footnotes") in the archive
  treatment — engraved archive divider + "ARCHIVE · NOTES 1–388" lockup, 12
  chapter note-headers (290/318/346/392/462/489/544/569/609/740/781/825) as mono
  ledger subheads with hairlines, note paras at 0.9rem Source Serif with hanging
  numerals, 60ch, faint Ledger panel behind the tail only, colophon end-mark
  (broken-key glyph) closing the book. No ornaments inside the tail.
- **Epigraphs:** p1c9 Pfaff (paras 0–2), p1c10 Pfaff (0–1), p1c11 Tanzi (para 0)
  in copper rule frames with Plex Mono attribution; p1c11 appendix proverb (251)
  as epigraph, not head. Quote-led openers already carry `.no-drop` (shared).
- **Reduced motion:** plates are static line-work; only assay-bar fill and
  hover/focus states animate — all disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.
- **Responsive:** plate-left ≥900px; banner ≤900px (max-height 320px, plates
  never hidden, S1); Bodoni weight bump at 390px (S6); footnote panel collapses
  to inline at <1100px per draft §3.
