# Art Direction — Napoleon: A Life

## Identity

Campaign-map parchment, muted imperial gold, and charcoal. A compass rose and bicorne silhouette anchor the cover without turning the book into pageantry.

## Cover and chapter plates

Carry the three source parts—Rise, Mastery, and Denouement—through restrained map, dispatch, and campaign motifs. Keep chapter titles and part divisions exact to the printed contents.

## Reader integration

- Keep the cover artwork in `art/cover.svg` and its `design/chapters.json` entry on the shared reader art slot.
- Chapter entries use the stable chapter IDs in `data/toc.json`; captions and motifs must stay attached to those IDs.
- Keep contrast, proportions, and chapter typography consistent with the existing `theme.css` and shared reader controls.
