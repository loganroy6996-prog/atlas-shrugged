Grammar recap (ArtDirector F7–F8 compliant — NO index arithmetic, NO odd/even
parity; every value below is authored):
- Numeral fill % tracks the part HEAT ARC (Keating 0–20 → Toohey 15–45 →
  Wynand 45–100 → Roark 70–100) with authored per-chapter overrides; climaxes
  solid (100%). S = stroke-only base, H = heat-filled, ● = solid part-accent.
- Banner bar compositions are authored per chapter from this vocabulary:
  STRATA (3 horizontal bars low — rock, sea, courses), HIGHBARS (3 horizontal
  high — headlines, cornices), DRILL (vertical bars — drill, rain, mullions,
  jets), SINGLE-HI / SINGLE-LO (one pronouncement / threshold line),
  SPLIT (bars both flanks — two figures facing), HEARTH (full-width hearth
  line — climaxes only), ASCENT (rising bars — p4c20 one-off).
- Drop-cap part-accent except dialogue-cold opens (Bronze, marked COLD).
