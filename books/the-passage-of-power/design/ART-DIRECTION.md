# Art Direction — The Passage of Power

## Identity

Deep blue, brass, and White House limestone. The cover follows a transfer of authority through rooms, papers, and an abrupt change of office.

## Cover and chapter plates

Maintain the five printed parts. Favor thresholds, briefing pages, desk lamps, and presidential seals used sparingly; the visual arc moves from campaign to succession.

## Reader integration

- Keep the cover artwork in `art/cover.svg` and its `design/chapters.json` entry on the shared reader art slot.
- Chapter entries use the stable chapter IDs in `data/toc.json`; captions and motifs must stay attached to those IDs.
- Keep contrast, proportions, and chapter typography consistent with the existing `theme.css` and shared reader controls.
